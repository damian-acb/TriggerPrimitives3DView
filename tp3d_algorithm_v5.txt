tp3d v5: 3D space points from trigger primitives (dune10kt 1x2x6)
=================================================================

Code: aiml/improved_v5/tp3d.py
      reconstruct(tp, dataset) runs the whole chain.

The reconstruction uses no MC truth. Most settings were chosen without truth (the
chi2 model, the time-shifted sideband). The exceptions are:
  - the v3 ADC cut values, from the truth-based ADC study in check_AtmCosRad/main.py;
  - the five v5 settings, which were tuned with truth (allowed for this version) on
    separate events (run 21373 files 000223-000227) and evaluated on others
    (files 000001-000020).

v5 = v3 (improved_v3, unchanged) + five changes against gaps along tracks:
  - chain seeds (step 7f): for tracks parallel to the APA, which have no
    unambiguous seeds;
  - sharing limit 3 -> 10 (step 7d): for tracks parallel to one set of induction
    wires;
  - segment growth (step 7b): for tracks along the drift, whose long TPs give
    sparse points;
  - cluster-aware ADC cut (step 8): the cut no longer removes small hits on tracks
    and showers;
  - growth reach 6 -> 10 cm (step 7b): recovers points just beyond 6 cm (big hits,
    sparse stretches of tracks and showers).

Everything else, including all calibrations, is identical to v3. Section 7 describes
how the gaps were diagnosed, section 8 how the settings were chosen, and section 9
the results.

1. TERMINOLOGY
--------------

TP
    A trigger primitive: one hit on one channel. The algorithm uses its channel,
    time_start, samples_over_threshold, time_peak (= time_start + 32 *
    samples_to_peak, in DTS ticks; 32 ticks per ADC sample) and adc_integral.

U, V, X
    The three wire planes of an APA:
      - U and V are the induction planes (wires at +-35.71 deg from vertical);
      - X is the collection plane (vertical wires, fixed z).
    Each APA has U channels 0-799, V channels 800-1599, and X channels 1600-2079
    (x<0 face) and 2080-2559 (x>0 face).

Collection TP
    A TP on an X channel. Each 3D point is built around one collection TP,
    which gives z (the wire position) and x (from the drift time).

Candidate
    One possible match of a collection TP with U and/or V TPs. A collection TP
    can have several candidates.

3-plane point
    A candidate made of a U, a V and an X TP whose wires cross at the same
    height (|h_U - h_V| < 3 cm).

2-plane point
    A candidate made of an X TP and only one induction TP. It's used only when
    the collection TP has no 3-plane candidate. With one induction plane there
    is no consistency test, so it is weaker.

Ghost
    A wrong combination of wires that happen to cross: geometrically valid, but
    not hit by the same particle. Ghosts appear when several hits arrive at the
    same time (tracks parallel to the APA, showers, several particles at the
    same drift distance).

chi2
    The ranking score of a candidate:
      - time agreement of each induction TP;
      - charge agreement of each induction TP;
      - for 3-plane candidates, the height mismatch (h_U - h_V)^2;
      - for 2-plane candidates, a fixed penalty of 9.
    The time and charge widths are measured, so exp(-chi2/2) is used as a
    relative likelihood.

Cluster
    A group of TPs on neighbouring wires of the same plane (and the same X face)
    of one APA, whose time intervals [time_start, time_start + 32 *
    samples_over_threshold] overlap within 64 ticks. A track appears as one
    long cluster in each plane. Induction wires count as neighbours modulo 800,
    since they wrap around the APA frame.

Seed
    A collection TP whose point is accepted without needing neighbours. There
    are two kinds:
      - unique: exactly one candidate, and it is 3-plane;
      - prob_seed: the best candidate is 3-plane and holds >= 90 % of the
        model probability of all its candidates (step 7a).

Supported point
    A seed, a point grown from seeds through 3D neighbours, or a point bridged
    along a straight track. Supported points are the algorithm's main output.

Unsupported point
    A collection TP that still has candidates after ghost removal, but that no
    seed, growth or bridge reached. It keeps its best-chi2 candidate and is
    flagged support = False. It is still in the output, but less trusted.

stage
    Output column saying how a point was accepted:
      unique, prob_seed  seeds
      support            grown from a neighbour within 10 cm (6 cm before v5)
      bridge             accepted along a straight track
      best_chi2          unsupported

Time-shifted (accidental) sample
    The same events with the induction planes moved by 30000 ticks, so every
    match is accidental. It measures fakes without truth.

    It cannot see ghosts made of real hits from the same activity. Those need
    real-hit coincidences, which the shift destroys.

ADC cut (v3)
    Collection TPs with adc_integral below ADC_CUT_X (1300 for Simple
    Threshold, 3100 for AbsRS) get no 3D point in the output. Induction TPs are
    never cut.


2. INPUT, AND THE ADC CUT
-------------------------

The TP table of one TPG algorithm (tpmakerTPCsimpleThr or tpmakerTPCabsRS) of
the TriggerPrimitiveHDF5Dump output, with one row per TP.

**v3 applies one ADC cut, on collection TPs only** (step 8):

    adc_integral >= 1300  (Simple Threshold)
    adc_integral >= 3100  (AbsRS)

These are the "knee" values of the ADC study in check_AtmCosRad/main.py. There,
about 99 % of noise and about 93 % of radiological collection TPs are below
them, against about 29 % of the signal collection TPs, which carry less than 1 %
of the signal collection ADC.

All TPs are still used to build the 3D points. The cut only decides which points
are kept in the output; section 6 explains why.

The other charge-based selection is the charge-consistency test (step 4). It
compares induction and collection charge with each other; it is not a
threshold.


3. GEOMETRY AND CALIBRATION (unchanged from v1)
-----------------------------------------------

Geometry: dunecore GDML dune10kt_v6_refactored_1x2x6.
  - 12 APAs in 6 z columns (232.39 cm apart) and 2 rows;
  - even APAs are the bottom row, odd APAs the top row, rotated by 180 deg;
  - the induction wires wrap around the frame.

Unroll the frame into a cylinder of circumference 2W (W = 400 x 0.57477 cm):
s = z on the x<0 face, s = 2W - z on the x>0 face. Then each induction channel
c is a straight helix:

    c_U * DZ =  s + h * tan(theta) + aU   (mod 2W)
    c_V * DZ = -s + h * tan(theta) + aV   (mod 2W)

Here h is the height above the APA's readout end, DZ = 0.57477 cm and
theta = 35.71 deg. One induction channel plus the collection wire's z gives h
without ambiguity.

Position of a point:
  - x = +-(0.0025088 * time_peak + 3.00155) cm, from the drift;
  - z = the collection wire's z;
  - y from h and the APA row.

Calibrations, all measured without truth:

  timing offset dt (induction - collection peak time), calibrate.py:
      Simple Threshold  U -384, V -224 ticks
      AbsRS             U -160, V  -32 ticks
                        (run 21241, 1000 events; confirmed on 5000 events of 21373)

  AbsRS time windows, calibrate_windows.py, win_tpmakerTPCabsRS.json:
      a main peak and a lobe ~8 samples earlier, each with its own window
      (run 21373 files 000203-000222)

  charge model, calibrate_charge.py, qcal_<dataset>.json:
      median and width of ln(adc_ind / adc_X) in 12 bins of collection ADC
      (run 21373 files 000203-000222)


4. THE ALGORITHM, STEP BY STEP
------------------------------

Step 1. Derived quantities
    For each TP: APA, plane, wire number and X face. Each induction TP's time is
    moved to the collection clock: t_c = time_peak - dt[view].

Step 2. Time matching
    For each collection TP, collect the U and V TPs on the same APA whose t_c
    falls in a window:

    Simple Threshold: +-96 ticks.

    AbsRS: two windows per view.
        U: main  -80..+112 ticks, lobe -304..-208 ticks
        V: main  -80..+112 ticks, lobe -272..-176 ticks
    A match in the lobe gets a chi2 penalty of 2.5 (U) / 3.0 (V).

Step 3. Height from each induction match
    The helix equations give h for each matched induction TP. It must be inside
    the APA (h <= 603 cm).

Step 4. Charge consistency (Q_CUT = 2)
    r = ln(adc_ind / adc_X) must be within 2 widths of its expected value for
    that collection ADC. Its pull^2 is added to chi2.

Step 5. Candidates
    3-plane
        Every (U, V) pair with |h_U - h_V| < 3 cm, with h = (h_U + h_V) / 2.
        chi2 = ((h_U - h_V) / 1.4)^2 + time terms + charge terms.
    2-plane
        Only for collection TPs with no 3-plane candidate. Every single U or V
        match is kept, with chi2 = its time + charge terms + 9.

Step 6. Cluster consensus (v2, CL_FRAC = 0.35)
    TPs are clustered in each plane (see "Cluster"). Every 3-plane candidate
    votes for its (X cluster, U cluster, V cluster) combination.

    A real track gives many triplets that all combine the same three clusters.
    A ghost that pairs a hit of the right X cluster with hits of other U/V
    clusters collects few votes.

    In X clusters of at least 5 TPs, a 3-plane candidate is dropped if its
    combination has fewer than 35 % of the votes of the best combination of
    that X cluster. A collection TP always keeps at least its best-voted
    triplet.

Step 7. Ghost removal: one point per collection TP

    7a) Seeds (v2: probability seeds, SEED_PROB = 0.9)
        - unique seeds: collection TPs with exactly one candidate, which is
          3-plane;
        - probability seeds: collection TPs whose best candidate is 3-plane and
          has

              P_best = 1 / sum_j exp(-(chi2_j - chi2_best) / 2) >= 0.9

          over all their candidates j. For two candidates this means the best
          is better by a chi2 gap of at least 4.4.
        Seeds are accepted best chi2 first, respecting the sharing limit (7d).

    7b) Growth (v2: until nothing changes; v5: segment distance, SEG_GROWTH, and
        reach GROW_R = 10 cm)
        Every remaining collection TP takes its candidate closest to an accepted
        point within 10 cm (6 cm before v5; ties broken by chi2). This is repeated until a pass
        accepts nothing new; v1 stopped after 4 passes.

        v5: a collection TP is not a point in x but covers a stretch of drift:
        lenx = samples_over_threshold x 32 ticks x 0.0025088 cm/tick, around its
        peak. Growth now measures the distance between those stretches: the x
        separation minus half of both stretches (not below 0), combined with the
        y and z separation.

        On a track running along the drift, each collection wire sees one long
        pulse (7-9 cm of drift), and the peaks of consecutive wires are 5-6 cm
        apart even though the charge is continuous. With the peak-to-peak
        distance, growth stalled there.

    7c) Directional bridging (v2; BRIDGE_R = 30 cm, BRIDGE_PERP = 2.5 cm)
        When growth stops, a local track direction is computed for every
        accepted point: the principal axis of the accepted points within 8 cm.
        It is used only where those points are linear (largest-eigenvalue
        fraction >= 0.9, at least 4 points).

        A remaining candidate up to 30 cm from such a point, and within 2.5 cm
        of its track line, is accepted. The closest to the line is preferred,
        then the lowest chi2.

        Growth (7b) then resumes from the bridged points. 7b and 7c alternate
        until neither accepts anything. This crosses gaps in a straight track
        that 6 cm growth cannot jump, e.g. regions where the TPs are ambiguous,
        an induction plane is missing, or channels are merged.

    7d) Sharing limit (MAX_SHARE = 10 in v5, was 3; applied in 7a-7c)
        No induction TP may be used by more than 10 accepted points. A track
        running almost parallel to one set of induction wires leaves one long
        induction TP that crosses many collection wires (up to ~60), so 3 was
        blocking real tracks. When several
        collection TPs compete for the last slots, the better-ranked ones get
        them, and the others try their next candidate.

    7e) Leftovers
        Collection TPs never reached keep their best-chi2 candidate as an
        unsupported point (support = False).

    7f) Chain seeds (v5; CHAIN_R = 8 cm, CHAIN_K = 5, CHAIN_LIN = 0.9,
        CHAIN_ROUNDS = 4)
        After 7a-7e, take every 3-plane point (supported or best-chi2). For each
        unsupported one, look at its neighbours within 8 cm.

        If there are at least 5 of them and they lie on a straight line (largest
        eigenvalue fraction of their spread >= 0.9), the TP keeps only that
        candidate, which makes it a unique seed. Then 7a-7e run again. This is
        repeated up to 4 times.

        Tracks running nearly parallel to the APA plane have all their hits at
        almost the same drift time. Every collection TP then has many equally
        good U/V matches (median 9-169), so there are no unique or probability
        seeds, and growth never starts. The best-chi2 guesses there are still
        91-99 % correct and line up along the track, while ghosts scatter.


Step 8. ADC cut on the output (v3; ADC_CUT_X, CUT_STAGE = "after";
        v5: CUT_KEEP_CLUSTER = 10, CUT_LINK = 6 cm)
    Points whose collection TP has adc_integral below the cut are removed from
    the output: below 1300 for Simple Threshold, below 3100 for AbsRS.

    v5: except supported points that belong to a 3D cluster of at least 10
    supported points (points linked within 6 cm). Radiological and noise blips
    are isolated, so they are still removed. Small hits on tracks and showers
    (track edges, delta rays, low-charge wires) are kept; v3 removed them, which
    left visible holes in tracks (e.g. 44 correct muon points in event 43 with
    Simple Threshold).

    Steps 1-7 run on all TPs, so the small collection TPs still act as stepping
    stones for growth and bridging along tracks and showers. They are only
    dropped at the end.

    reconstruct(..., cut_stage="before") instead removes them before matching.
    That is faster, but loses signal (section 6). reconstruct(..., adc_cut=None)
    reproduces improved_v2 exactly.

    reconstruct(..., seg_growth=False, chain=False, max_share=3,
    cut_keep_cluster=None, radius=6) reproduces improved_v3 exactly (same points;
    v5 only adds the output column lenx).


Output: one row per collection TP with at least one candidate. The columns are:

    x, y, z
    nplanes
    support
    stage
    chi2
    iX, i0, i1 (TP indices of the X, U and V TP; -1 = plane missing)
    adc_integral, channel, time_peak


5. HOW THE v2 SETTINGS (unchanged in v3 and v5) WERE CHOSEN (no truth)
---------------------------------------------------------------

Tuning files: run 21373, files 000223-000242 (1000 events). The metric is the
ADC-weighted supported fraction in time minus the same fraction in the
time-shifted sample.

  - Growth until nothing changes: no setting to choose. AbsRS supported goes
    from 54.9 % to 61.1 % (accidental 5.7 % -> 7.2 %).

  - Cluster consensus: CL_FRAC from 0.1 to 0.5 all gave the same result
    (+0.2 points). 0.35 was kept. Most ambiguous (U, V) pairs on a track come
    from the same U and V clusters, so votes can rarely separate them.

  - Probability seeds: the time-shifted sample keeps improving as the threshold
    gets looser, all the way down. It can't see ghosts made of real hits, so
    looser always looks better there. The threshold was therefore set from the
    chi2 model rather than from the scan: 90 % model probability.

    For reference, AbsRS supported in time (and accidental):
        P >= 0.8    68.9 % (8.5 %)
        P >= 0.9    66.6 % (8.2 %)
        P >= 0.95   65.8 % (8.1 %)

  - Bridging: a 30 cm reach with a 2.5 cm corridor was best among
    {15, 30, 60 cm} x {1.0, 1.5, 2.5 cm}.

7. WHY TRACKS STILL HAD GAPS IN v3 (diagnosis)
----------------------------------------------

Examined: 151 muons (cosmic and atmospheric) in 450 AtmCosRad events, AbsRS. In v3,
75 % of their collection TPs had a correct supported point. A gap is >= 3
consecutive TPs along the track without one. Gaps by cause (number of TPs):

  candidates exist, but never reached (unsupported)   6156 TPs  (gaps up to 2 m)
  below the ADC cut                                   1258 TPs  (sub-cm gaps)
  all candidates removed by the charge cut             513 TPs
  supported but wrongly matched                        469 TPs
  no U/V match at all                                  148 TPs  (often APA edges)

The long "never reached" gaps came from three track orientations:

  (a) Parallel to the APA plane (dx ~ 0). All hits arrive at almost the same
      time, so each collection TP has many equally good candidates and there
      are no seeds. Best guesses there are 91-99 % correct. Fix: chain seeds
      (7f).
  (b) Parallel to one set of induction wires. One long induction TP serves up
      to ~60 collection TPs, and the limit of 3 rejected the real track. Fix:
      sharing limit 10 (7d).
  (c) Along the drift direction (e.g. file 000001, event 43). Long collection TPs
      (7-9 cm of drift each) put consecutive peaks 5-6 cm apart, at the edge of
      the growth reach. Fix: segment growth (7b).

In addition, the v3 ADC cut removed correct small hits on tracks. Fix:
cluster-aware cut (step 8).


8. HOW THE v5 SETTINGS WERE CHOSEN (with truth)
-----------------------------------------------

Tuning events: run 21373 files 000223-000227 (250 events), truth check as in
section 9.

  Chain seeds
      Prototyped at 6 cm / 8 neighbours / linearity 0.95 and at 8 cm / 5 / 0.9.
      The looser one recovers more (e.g. a muon with 202 of 399 TPs goes to
      370), without loss of purity.
  Sharing limit
      3, 6, 10, unlimited: 6 or more give the same signal. 10 was kept as a
      safety margin against ghost chains.
  Cluster-aware cut
      Minimum cluster size 3, 5, 10 or 20, or the plain cut. Signal ADC is the
      same for all (~83 % AbsRS). Smaller sizes keep more small signal hits but
      also more noise TPs (5: 11.5 noise TPs per event, 10: 9.2, 20: 7.9; plain
      cut: 1.9). 10 was chosen as the compromise.
  Segment growth
      On or off (on is better for every particle type, most for Simple
      Threshold cosmics).
  Growth reach
      Added after the first v5 evaluation, because a few high-ADC hits
      (e.g. in file 000001, event 43) were left unsupported 6-12 cm from the
      nearest supported point. Each extra 2 cm of reach recovers ~1 point of
      signal charge for ~0.2 points of purity.

      AbsRS, tuning events          6 cm   8 cm   10 cm   12 cm
        signal, correct supported   83.3   84.5   85.3    86.1
        signal, strictly correct    71.7   72.8   73.5    74.2
        cosmics                     87.4   89.2   90.5    92.3
        signal, wrongly matched      3.8    4.0    4.3     4.4
        purity of supported ADC     94.0   93.8   93.6    93.5

      Noise and the purity by TP count do not change. 10 cm was chosen: most of
      the gain, with diminishing returns beyond.

Reading the numbers:
  - v5 recovers 5-8 points of the signal charge and 10-12 points of the signal
    hits, at nearly the same ADC purity (94.1 % against 94.7 % for AbsRS; 90.6 %
    against 91.2 % for ST). The gain is largest where v3 left gaps:
      - cosmics: +6 to +9 points;
      - marley: +15 to +17 points;
      - low-energy iBDM electrons: +7 to +12 points.
  - Chain seeds give the largest share, then the 10 cm reach, segment growth
    and the sharing limit.
  - The price of the cluster-aware cut is noise: about 10 (AbsRS) / 21 (ST) noise
    TPs per event survive inside large clusters, i.e. next to real activity.
    They are 0.1 % of the supported charge but lower the purity by TP count from
    80 % to 77 % (AbsRS). With the plain v3 cut instead (cut_keep_cluster=None),
    v5 keeps almost all its signal gain in ADC and v3's TP purity, but loses the
    small hits on tracks again.
  - The accidental rate in the time-shifted sample rises by ~4 points, but the
    truth check shows radiological points almost unchanged (5.6 -> 5.8 % of
    their ADC). Most of what the shifted sample counts are induction hits of
    real activity paired with unrelated collection hits, which do not happen in
    time.


10. WHAT STILL LIMITS IT
------------------------

  - Showers (atmospherics): 84-85 % correct by the loose definition, but only
    66-67 % strictly. Inside showers several hits share drift time and particle.
    The best next step is an induction "charge budget" (as in LArSoft's
    SpacePointSolver): all points that use one induction TP must add up to its
    charge.
  - Remaining small gaps: hits whose candidates all fail the charge cut (large
    hits, where the charge model is coarse; see improved_v4), hits with no U/V
    match (often APA edges), and a few wrong matches.
  - Noise next to activity is kept by the cluster-aware cut. A cut on the noise
    TP's own shape (short, low peak) could remove it without touching signal.
  - Speed: ~75 ms/event in Python. For real time, the candidate step and the
    chain-seed rounds are the first to optimise (vectorise across APAs, numba,
    or C++).
