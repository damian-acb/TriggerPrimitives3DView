tp3d v6: 3D space points from trigger primitives (dune10kt 1x2x6)
=================================================================

Code: tp3d.py, reconstruct(tp, dataset) runs the whole chain (development copy:
aiml/improved_v6/tp3d.py). Plan written before the code: aiml/improved_v6/tp3d_v6_plan.txt.
Scans and checks: aiml/tune_truth/E_v6/ (check_v6.py, scan_share.py, final_eval.py,
final_eval.txt) and aiml/tune_truth/D_recovery/.

The reconstruction uses no MC truth. Truth was used to choose the v6 settings and to
score them. Signal = atmospherics + cosmics (marley is reported on its own).

v6 = v5 + four changes that keep more energy:
  1. U-V height tolerance 3.0 -> 1.25 cm (H_TOL)
  2. charge-test rescue for long collection TPs (RESCUE_LEN = 5 cm)
  3. promotion of unsupported points in 2D collection clusters of >= 5 TPs
     (PROMOTE_CL = 5)
  4. charge budget of shared induction TPs (BUDGET_Q = 0.5)
Tried and left off (no gain): track-shaped sharing (SHARE_FREE) and cluster-level
matching (CM_OVERLAP, CM_QCUT). New optional output: split_long (several points per
long collection TP).
With h_tol=3.0, rescue_len=None, promote_cl=None, budget_q=None, v6 gives exactly the
v5 points (checked, check_v6.py).


=====================================================================
PART A. THE SHORT VERSION
=====================================================================

A collection wire gives z (which wire) and x (when the charge arrived). The height y
comes from the two induction planes, U and V, whose tilted wires cross the
collection wire at different heights.

  1. Read all TPs of the event.
  2. For each collection hit, find the U and V hits of the same APA that arrived at
     the same time; each gives a possible height.
  3. Keep U-V pairs that give the same height (within 1.25 cm) and whose charge fits
     the collection charge. Each pair is a candidate 3D point; usually one is real
     and the others are ghosts.
     NEW: a long collection hit (a track heading toward the APA) that loses all its
     candidates to the charge test gets them back without that test.
  4. Accept the sure ones first (seeds).
  5. Grow: every other hit takes its candidate nearest to an accepted point (10 cm);
     jump small gaps along straight tracks; start chains on lined-up best guesses.
     NEW: a U or V hit may be shared by several collection hits only while their
     charges still add up to roughly its own charge (charge budget). A ghost that
     reuses an induction hit already "spent" on real hits is refused.
  6. Hits never reached keep their best guess, "unsupported".
     NEW: an unsupported point in a group of >= 5 neighbouring collection hits
     becomes supported.
  7. Drop small collection hits (ADC cut) unless they sit in a large 3D cluster.

Result on 1000 new events (% of atmospheric + cosmic energy in correctly matched
supported points):  AbsRS 77.8 -> 84.4,  Simple Threshold 75.5 -> 80.2,
with slightly higher purity.


=====================================================================
PART B. THE FULL VERSION
=====================================================================

B1. What did not change
-----------------------
Input, geometry, calibrations (timing offsets, AbsRS windows, charge model), cluster
consensus, seeds, segment growth (10 cm), bridging, chain seeds, the sharing limit of
10 and the cluster-aware ADC cut after the reconstruction are exactly as in v5
(tp3d_algorithm_v5.txt, sections 1-4).

B2. Change 1: h_tol 3.0 -> 1.25 cm (candidates)
------------------------------------------------
A 3-plane candidate needs |h_U - h_V| < h_tol. With truth, real U-V crossings agree
within 0.4 cm for 73 % of TPs (rms 0.46 cm); ghosts are spread evenly to 3 cm. At
1.25 cm more than half of the ghosts go and ~7 % of real crossings are lost; those
TPs fall back to 2-plane candidates, where growth usually still finds the right one.
Scanned: h_tol 1.0-5.0 x height resolution 0.3-3.0 (tune_truth/B_match_tol). The
height resolution (1.4 cm) and the 2-plane penalty (9) stay.
Cost: more radiological charge is supported (5.8 -> 8.7 % of its ADC, AbsRS).

B3. Change 2: charge-test rescue (rescue_candidates)
----------------------------------------------------
A collection TP with drift length lenx >= 5 cm that has no candidate after the charge
test gets its candidates rebuilt without the test (the charge pull stays in chi2).
Output column "rescue" = True for these points.
Why: all of the v5 "no match" energy was lost to the charge test, not to missing
induction TPs (the owner particle was in both a U and a V TP every time). These TPs
are long (energy-weighted typical ~35 cm of drift, ADC ~40k for AbsRS): tracks
heading toward the APA, whose bipolar induction signal partly cancels, so the U/V
charge is far below the model.
Effect: no match 4.2 -> 0.6 % (AbsRS), 3.1 -> 0.9 % (ST); atmospheric primaries gain
most. No extra radiological or noise points.

B4. Change 3: cluster promotion (promote_clusters)
--------------------------------------------------
After the ADC cut, an unsupported point becomes supported (stage "cluster") if its
collection TP belongs to a 2D collection cluster (tp3d.clusters) of >= 5 collection
TPs. ~75 % of unsupported signal energy was already within 2 cm of the true height;
growth just never reached it. Radiological deposits cover 1-2 wires and rarely pass.
3 TPs would add ~40 % more supported radiological charge; 10 gains little.

B5. Change 4: charge budget of shared induction TPs (_Share, _accept)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Every accepted point uses one U and/or one V TP. Each induction TP keeps the sum of
the collection ADC of the points using it. A new point may use an induction TP that
already has users only if
    pull = (ln(q_ind / sum q_X) - mu(ln sum q_X)) / sig(ln sum q_X) >= -0.5,
with mu, sig the calibrated charge model and the sum including the new point. If not,
that candidate is refused and the collection TP tries its next one (as with the
sharing limit). Rescued candidates are exempt. When several TPs compete in one pass,
the better-ranked ones are charged first.
Why: wrong matches are the largest loss left (12-17 % of the signal energy), mostly in
atmospheric showers, and points whose induction TPs are shared carry about twice the
wrong-match rate of unshared ones (16-19 % vs 9 % for AbsRS). Physically one induction
wire sees the charge of all the collection hits it pairs with; a ghost reusing it
overdraws.
Scan (tune_truth/E_v6/scan_share.py, files 000243-000247; kept / purity):
    cut         AbsRS          ST
    off        84.64/86.27    79.90/80.23
    1.5        85.07/86.68    80.04/80.41
    1.0        85.13/86.75    80.08/80.51

    0.0        84.99/86.77    80.17/80.93

Tighter than 0.5 starts refusing real hits (unsupported rises). It also lowers the
radiological and noise points (ST: -5 % radiological charge).
Cost: time (B9).

B6. Tried, left off
-------------------
Track-shaped sharing (SHARE_FREE, SHARE_LIN): beyond N users, the users of an
induction TP must lie on a line. No effect for N = 1-3, linearity 0.8-0.9: with few
users almost any set of points is a line, and induction TPs with > 3 users carry only
~7 % of the energy.
Cluster-level matching (cluster_match: time-span overlap and summed-charge test of the
(X, U, V) clusters of each candidate): +-0.03 points. When it fails a candidate it
usually fails all candidates of that TP (which then keeps them), and the vote-based
cluster consensus already covers the rest.
Both are in the code, off by default.

B7. Optional output: split_long
-------------------------------
reconstruct(..., split_long=L): a collection TP with lenx > L cm becomes
ceil(lenx / L) points spread evenly over its drift segment (time_start to
time_start + 32 x samples_over_threshold), same y and z, each with an equal share of
adc_integral; column "part". The total charge is unchanged. Off by default; it does
not change which TPs are kept, only where their charge sits in 3D.

B9. Speed (one core, file 000268, 20 events)
--------------------------------------------
  AbsRS              v5 79 -> v6 88 ms/event
  Simple Threshold   v5 62 -> v6 100 ms/event
The charge budget accounts for the whole increase (without it v6 runs at 71 / 61):
refused candidates make the acceptance loop run more passes. First thing to optimise
if speed matters.

B10. What still limits it
-------------------------
  - Wrong matches in atmospheric showers (12 / 16 % of the signal energy) remain the
    largest loss, and the position score shows they are mostly real misplacements.
    The greedy charge budget helps a little; a global solution (all points that use
    an induction TP share its charge, solved together, as in LArSoft's
    SpacePointSolver) is the natural next step.
  - h_tol 1.25 lets more radiological charge through (+3 points of its ADC).
  - The rescue is a stand-in for a charge model that depends on the TP length.
